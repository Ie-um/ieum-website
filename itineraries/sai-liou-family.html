Two Days in Seoul, for Sai and Family

A glimpse of two days within your December stay, not your arrival or departure day, those we will shape precisely once your flights are set.

December 2026 Sai Liou, with two children Private driver throughout

It was such a pleasure speaking with you. Below is a small taste of what your December in Seoul could look like, one morning built entirely around you, one built around your family together in hanbok. I have included a few real images of each place so you can start to picture yourself there, not just read about it. Everything here can move, nothing is fixed until you tell me what you love.

Day One

Namsan, Skincare, and a Little Chaos for Everyone

A morning together, an afternoon apart, an evening together again.

Morning

Up to Namsan

While the city is still cool and the light is clearest, up to Namsan for the view that makes Seoul feel like a single, sweeping thing rather than a hundred separate neighborhoods. An easy, unhurried way to begin the day together.

Midday

Bibimbap, All Together

Lunch nearby, at a small Michelin Guide recommended bibimbap house near Namsan, warm rice and vegetables everyone builds their own way, easy for the kids and satisfying for you and your husband too, no unfamiliar flavors to talk anyone into.

Afternoon

A Morning of Skincare, Your Choice

Two directions, and we'll pick whichever speaks to you more closer to your trip. Either a facial rooted in traditional hanbang medicine, at one of Seoul's well regarded Korean herbal skincare houses, or something more targeted, a non invasive treatment for hyperpigmentation at a dermatology clinic. Either way, an hour set aside just for you.

Afternoon, in Parallel

The Kids Meet the Cats

Meanwhile, your husband and children settle into a Myeongdong cat cafe, home to dozens of resident cats and an open afternoon of coffee, treats, and no itinerary of their own to keep to.

Late Afternoon

Myeongdong, Together Again

The family reunites along Myeongdong's shopping streets, Korean cosmetics, fashion, and street snacks at every turn, easy to wander with kids since there's always something new a few steps ahead.

Evening

Cheese Dakgalbi, to Close the Day

Dinner together, spicy stir fried chicken pulled through melted cheese at the table, the kind of hands on, slightly messy meal that tends to be everyone's favorite of the whole trip.

A note from Doha, this skincare hour is placed before your photography day on purpose. Skin settles beautifully in the day after a treatment, so by the time you're in hanbok at the palace, you'll look and feel your very best.

Day Two

Hanbok at the Palace

One signature morning, given the room to be fully savored.

Morning

Private Hanbok Fitting

Your driver brings the family to a hanbok house near the palace, where you and the children are fitted for traditional Korean dress, chosen together, colors and all.

Late Morning

Gyeongbokgung Palace

In hanbok, through Korea's grandest palace, its courtyards and gates a backdrop few travelers ever get to walk through dressed as part of the scene rather than simply visiting it.

1 Hour

Private Snap Photography

A professional photographer joins for one hour within the palace grounds, so the day becomes something you carry home, not just something you remember.

Midday

Traditional Korean Lunch, Near the Palace

A quiet, family run restaurant tucked into a hanok not far from the palace, known for one dish alone, samgyetang, whole ginseng chicken simmered until it falls off the bone. A gentle, warming introduction to Korean food that tends to win kids over as easily as adults.

Afternoon

A Wander Through Seochon

Narrow hanok alleys just west of the palace, small galleries, quiet cafes, and the city's oldest bookstore tucked among them. Easy walking, still in hanbok if you like, no fixed stops, just time to drift.

A note from Doha, these are two days from within your five, not your arrival or departure day. I would shape those, along with your remaining time, entirely around your flights and whatever you and the children want more of, culture, wellness, or simply room to breathe.
