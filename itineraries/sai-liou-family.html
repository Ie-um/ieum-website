A Sample Journey

Two Days in Seoul, for Sai and Family

A glimpse of two days within your December stay, not your arrival or departure day, those we will shape precisely once your flights are set.

December 2026 Sai Liou, with two children Private driver throughout

It was such a pleasure speaking with you. Below is a small taste of what your December in Seoul could look like, one morning built entirely around you, one built around your family together in hanbok. I have included a few real images of each place so you can start to picture yourself there, not just read about it. Everything here can move, nothing is fixed until you tell me what you love.

Day One

Myeongdong, Beauty and a Little Chaos for Everyone

A morning for you, an afternoon for all of you together.

Morning

A Signature Facial, at O HUI&WHOO Spa

In the heart of Myeongdong, a facial built around OHUI and Whoo, the same premium Korean skincare houses favored by Seoul's own actors and stars. An hour set aside just for you, before anything else on the trip asks for your attention.

Morning, in Parallel

The Kids Meet the Cats

Just steps away, your husband and children settle into Roof Cat Me Café, home to around fifty resident cats and an open afternoon of coffee, treats, and no itinerary of their own to keep to.

Afternoon

Myeongdong, Together Again

The family reunites along Myeongdong's shopping streets, Korean cosmetics, fashion, and street snacks at every turn, easy to wander with kids since there's always something new a few steps ahead.

Evening

A Relaxed Family Dinner

Dinner nearby in Myeongdong, unhurried, closing out a day built around you as much as the children.

A note from Doha, this spa morning is placed before your photography day on purpose. Skin settles beautifully in the day after a facial, so by the time you're in hanbok at the palace, you'll look and feel your very best.

Day Two

Hanbok at the Palace

One signature morning, given the room to be fully savored.

Morning

Private Hanbok Fitting

Your driver brings the family to a hanbok house near the palace, where you and the children are fitted for traditional Korean dress, chosen together, colors and all.

Late Morning

Gyeongbokgung Palace

In hanbok, through Korea's grandest palace, its courtyards and gates a backdrop few travelers ever get to walk through dressed as part of the scene rather than simply visiting it.

1 Hour

Private Snap Photography

A professional photographer joins for one hour within the palace grounds, so the day becomes something you carry home, not just something you remember.

Midday

Traditional Korean Lunch, at Tosokchon

Samgyetang, whole ginseng chicken soup simmered until it falls apart, served in a quiet hanok setting near the palace. A gentle, warming introduction to Korean food that tends to win kids over as easily as adults.

Afternoon

A Wander Through Seochon

Narrow hanok alleys just west of the palace, small galleries, quiet cafes, and the city's oldest bookstore tucked among them. Easy walking, still in hanbok if you like, no fixed stops, just time to drift.

A note from Doha, these are two days from within your five, not your arrival or departure day. I would shape those, along with your remaining time, entirely around your flights and whatever you and the children want more of, culture, wellness, or simply room to breathe.
