A Sample Journey

Two Days in Seoul, for Sai and Family

A first glimpse of how we would shape your December stay, beginning where you land, at the Grand Hyatt Seoul.

December 2026 Sai Liou, with two children Private driver throughout
Day One

Arrival, and Hannam-dong at Your Own Pace

Nothing far from the hotel, nothing rushed. A day meant for settling in.

Morning

Private Airport Transfer & Check-In

Your driver meets you on arrival and brings you directly to the Grand Hyatt Seoul, in Hannam-dong, where the whole trip begins without a single logistic left for you to think about.

Afternoon

Banyan Tree Club & Spa Seoul

A short private drive to Banyan Tree Club & Spa, ten minutes from the hotel, where a dedicated kids' pool and kids' club give the children their own afternoon, while you slip into the spa for a quiet hour of your own. A rare kind of afternoon where everyone in the family is happy at once.

Evening

Namsan, and a Welcome Dinner

Up to Namsan as the sky turns, for the kind of skyline view that makes a first evening in Seoul feel like it was worth the flight, followed by a relaxed family dinner nearby in Hannam-dong.

A note from Doha, this first day is intentionally unhurried. Families arriving from long flights do best with a light landing, not a packed one.

Day Two

Hanbok at the Palace

One signature morning, given the room to be fully savored.

Morning

Private Hanbok Fitting

Your driver brings the family to a hanbok house near the palace, where you and the children are fitted for traditional Korean dress, chosen together, colors and all.

Late Morning

Gyeongbokgung Palace

In hanbok, through Korea's grandest palace, its courtyards and gates a backdrop few travelers ever get to walk through dressed as part of the scene rather than simply visiting it.

1 Hour

Private Snap Photography

A professional photographer joins for one hour within the palace grounds, so the day becomes something you carry home, not just something you remember.

A note from Doha, this is only the beginning of what your five days could hold. The remaining three days, I would shape entirely around what you and the children love most, whether that's more culture, more wellness, or simply more time to breathe.

Let's Continue This

This Was Only a Glimpse

If this feels like the beginning of your trip, I would love to sit down and design the remaining days together, built entirely around your family.
