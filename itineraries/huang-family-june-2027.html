Arrival

Arrive rested, and be met at the door

In the air

First class suites to Seoul

Korean Air's first class is a private suite with a sliding door, a fully flat bed and dining on your own schedule. It flies nonstop to Seoul from major cities in the United States and Europe, such as New York, Los Angeles and Chicago.

Plan ahead

Five seats together

First class has only a handful of seats on each aircraft, so seating five together means reserving well ahead. June 2027 flights are already open for booking.

On the ground

A private Sprinter from Incheon

Your chauffeur meets you in arrivals and takes the whole family and your luggage straight to the hotel in one spacious van, with child seats arranged if needed.

Where to stay

Family suites at Seoul's finest addresses

For a month, space matters as much as service. Each offers suites large enough for five and a pool for warm June afternoons. We would request long stay rates and confirm June availability.

Jamsil

Signiel Seoul

High in Lotte World Tower, with spacious suites and sweeping city views. Lotte World and its aquarium are steps away, a gift for younger travelers.

Gwanghwamun

Four Seasons Seoul

In the historic heart of the city, a short walk from Gyeongbokgung Palace, with an indoor pool and some of Seoul's best restaurants under one roof.

Namsan

Grand Hyatt Seoul

On the green slopes of Namsan, with an outdoor pool in summer, generous suites and space to breathe above the city.

The month

Seoul as home, with a few chapters beyond

Jeju

Beaches, volcanic trails, tea fields and private villas on Korea's island.

Busan

The coast, seafood markets and temples above the waves, under three hours by train.

Gyeongju

The thousand year old royal capital, with temples, tomb gardens and quiet countryside.

Gangwon

Mountain valleys, clear streams and the east coast, a cool escape from the city in summer.

June weather. Early June is warm and bright. The rainy season usually begins in the last week of June, so we plan the outdoor highlights first and keep wonderful indoor options ready for wet days.

Wellness

Rest, the Korean way

Wellness is at the heart of every IEUM journey: moments of stillness woven between the busier days.

A sound bath on traditional instruments

A private session with Wacheongjae, whose deep, resonant geomungo, a string instrument played in Korea for over a thousand years, calms the body and quiets the mind. Arranged privately for your family.

A night at a mountain temple

A temple stay with tea, meditation and the morning bell.

Hot springs and forest bathing

Mineral springs and guided walks in Korea's healing forests.

Korean spa and beauty rituals

Skincare consultations, facials and the bathhouse traditions Korea is known for.
