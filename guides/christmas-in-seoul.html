Christmas in Seoul 2026

Seoul takes Christmas to heart. From November the department stores become light shows, Christmas villages rise across the city, the great hotels release cakes that are gone within days, and on a fortunate night snow settles on the palace roofs. Here is the season in 2026, and how to enjoy it privately.

Updated 8 November 2026

25 December is a public holiday in Korea. Christmas Eve is the busiest night of the year for restaurants and hotels.

See the lights in Myeongdong

The two department store flagships facing each other in Myeongdong turn their buildings into Seoul's best known Christmas show. Go after five, when the city is dark and the screens come alive, and walk between them.

The Shinsegae media facade

Confirmed for 2026A three minute, forty second Christmas film plays across the whole facade of the Shinsegae flagship, made with 3D anamorphic effects that seem to step off the building. It runs daily from 5 pm to midnight, from 6 November 2026 to 31 January 2027.

Lotte Town Light

Confirmed for 2026Opposite, the Lotte flagship has a new media facade this year, 20,000 bulbs on its walls and about 100 metres of lit street from Euljiro 1-ga station to Avenuel.

Step into The Hyundai's Christmas village

The Hyundai Seoul in Yeouido stages the city's most anticipated Christmas installation. This year's theme is Christmas in New York City in 1985: thirteen ten metre buildings, neon signs and yellow taxis on the fifth floor Sounds Forest.

Dates, hours and booking

Confirmed for 2026Open 10 November to 31 December 2026, 10:30 am to 7:30 pm, closed on 18 and 26 December. Entry is free. Visits from 10 to 25 December are booked through The Hyundai Hi app, with up to four places per account. International visitors can book through Klook or use a dedicated waiting line on the day.

Walk the Christmas markets

Seoul's Christmas markets have grown into full European style villages, with timber stalls, carousels and mulled drinks, and each department store now runs its own.

Lotte Town Christmas Market, Jamsil

Confirmed for 2026A nineteenth century English Christmas street on the World Park lawn beside Lotte World Tower: a seventeen metre clock tower, a two storey carousel, a giant tree and about forty stalls of warm food and mulled drinks. A light and music show plays on Lotte World Mall every evening from 6 pm, with live carols at weekends and on Christmas Day. 19 November 2026 to 3 January 2027.

Shinsegae Holiday Market

Confirmed for 2026Christmas gifts and decorations in a brick house village along a tree lined path. At the Gangnam store from 30 October to 28 December, and at the Myeongdong flagship from 24 November to 31 December.

Gwanghwamun and the Han River

2026 dates to be confirmedThe city's own Gwanghwamun Market, a European style Christmas village on Gwanghwamun Square, is planned for December, with several listings pointing to an opening on 4 December. The Romantic Hangang Christmas Market at Ttukseom Hangang Park is planned for 18 to 27 December.

Spend a day at Lotte World

Lotte World, the indoor and outdoor theme park in Jamsil, dresses its castle for Christmas and runs a winter parade, with the Christmas market and Lotte World Tower a few steps away. It makes a full day for families, ending with the view from the tower's observation deck.

Order a hotel Christmas cake

In Korea a Christmas cake is the centrepiece of the day, and the hotels release limited editions each year that are gone within days. This season's collections take their cue from gift boxes, white trees and snow falling on a winter town. Pre-orders open in November, often with an early booking discount, for collection in the week before Christmas.

Skate and walk the lights downtown

From early December the old centre becomes one long evening walk: Gwanghwamun Square, the stream that runs east from it, and Seoul Plaza with its open air ice rink. In 2025 the Seoul Lantern Festival lined the Cheonggye stream with around 500 lantern sculptures.

Seoul Winter Festa

2026 dates to be confirmedThe city's winter festival, with light shows at Gwanghwamun and six other sites, is planned for 4 December 2026 to 31 January 2027, closing the year with the bell at Bosingak at midnight.

2026 dates to be confirmedLast season the rink opened on 19 December and cost ₩1,000 an hour, skates included, with tickets split between online booking and the door.

See the palaces in snow

Seoul is at its most beautiful the morning after a snowfall: Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung the morning after a snowfall, white on the curved roofs and quiet in the courtyards. Seoul's first snow of 2026 came early, on 8 November, and snow that settles is most likely from mid December. We keep one morning free and move it to meet the snow.

Take a day out of the city

An hour and a half from Seoul, the Garden of Morning Calm in Gapyeong lights its gardens for winter with millions of small lights, at their best on a clear night after snow. Last season it ran from 5 December to mid March, lit from around 5 pm.

Closer in, the granite peaks of Bukhansan and Dobongsan on the city's northern edge turn white after a heavy snowfall, a morning's walk with a mountain guide and a hot lunch below.

For snow you can count on, go further east to Gangwon: a night at Woljeongsa temple in Pyeongchang, the ski resorts, and a hot spring at dusk. Our Winter Retreat and Wellness Retreat are built around it.

Go to Christmas Mass

Myeongdong Cathedral, the mother church of Korean Catholicism, holds an English Mass every Sunday at 9 in the morning. Its Christmas Eve Mass is in Korean and very crowded, so arrive at least an hour early. We confirm the Christmas schedule with the cathedral for your dates.

Plan around a busy Christmas

25 December is a public holiday, and Christmas in Korea is a night for couples and friends more than a family day. Christmas Eve is one of the most reserved nights of the year: hotel dining rooms and the city's tasting menus fill weeks ahead, and the Christmas villages queue from opening. A private driver and tables held in advance turn the busiest week of the year into an easy one. See our guide to Korea in December for the weather, ski season and New Year.

Ask the common questions

Is Christmas a public holiday in Korea?

Yes. 25 December is a national public holiday in Korea. It is celebrated less as a family day and more as a night out for couples and friends, so restaurants, hotel dining rooms and Christmas markets are at their busiest on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

What is there to do in Seoul at Christmas?

See the light shows on the Shinsegae and Lotte department stores in Myeongdong, walk the Christmas village at The Hyundai Seoul and the Christmas market in Jamsil, watch The Nutcracker, order a hotel Christmas cake, skate at Seoul Plaza, and visit the palaces after a snowfall. For a day out, the Garden of Morning Calm lights up its gardens for winter.

When do the Christmas markets open in Seoul in 2026?

The Hyundai Seoul's Christmas village runs from 10 November to 31 December, the Lotte Town Christmas Market in Jamsil from 19 November to 3 January, and the Shinsegae Holiday Market from 30 October to 28 December in Gangnam and from 24 November to 31 December in Myeongdong. The city's Gwanghwamun market and the Romantic Hangang Christmas Market are planned for December, with final dates to be announced.

Do I need to book The Hyundai Seoul Christmas village?

For the busiest weeks, yes. Entry is free, but visits from 10 to 25 December are booked through The Hyundai Hi app, with up to four places per account. International visitors can also book through Klook or use a dedicated waiting line on the day.

Why are hotel Christmas cakes so popular in Korea?

A Christmas cake is the centrepiece of a Korean Christmas, and the hotels release limited editions each year that sell out quickly. In 2026 Pullman Ambassador Seoul Eastpole, Four Seasons Hotel Seoul and Le Méridien Seoul Myeongdong offer collections from about ₩78,000 to ₩150,000, while the most coveted cakes from the grand hotels reached ₩300,000 to ₩500,000 in 2025. Most open pre-orders in November, often with an early booking discount, for collection in the week before Christmas.

Does it snow in Seoul at Christmas?

Sometimes, but it is never guaranteed. Seoul's first snow of 2026 came early, on 8 November, and snow that settles is most likely from mid December to February. For snow you can count on, add a few days in the mountains of Gangwon.

Is there an English Christmas Mass in Seoul?

Myeongdong Cathedral holds an English Mass every Sunday at 9 in the morning. Its Christmas Eve Mass is in Korean and very crowded, so arrive at least an hour early. Check the cathedral's schedule for any English Mass on Christmas Day itself.
