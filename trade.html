Why IEUM

Korea is the request you keep hearing. We answer it with stillness.

IEUM is a private wellness concierge for Korea, between Seoul and New York. Our journeys move at the pace of the country's oldest rituals of rest: a night in a mountain temple, the low voice of the geomungo in a candlelit hall, an hour with a doctor of Korean medicine, tea poured by a master.

Much of this Korea opens only through introduction. Kitchens that take bookings in Korean alone, temples that receive guests by invitation, artisans who welcome few visitors. These are the relationships we keep, and we open them to your clients.

You remain their advisor throughout. We work quietly behind you, with one contact for you, a private driver and interpreter for them, and someone on hand in Korea at every hour.

Our journeys

Four journeys, composed for one guest at a time

Each begins from a blank page and takes its shape from the people travelling.

i.

Wellness, at the core

For guests who wish to return restored

Rest as Korea has practised it for centuries, and as few places can offer it.

  • A sound bath played live on the geomungo, Korea's oldest string instrument
  • A night in a mountain temple, waking to the dawn chant
  • An hour with a doctor of Korean medicine, then the warmth of a pine fired kiln
The Wellness Retreat
ii.

Plant based, by tradition

For vegan, vegetarian and food led guests

For more than a thousand years, Korea's temple kitchens have cooked only from the field and the mountain.

  • Meals shared with the monastics, in the temples where they cook
  • Jang, the fermented heart of Korean food, with the families who still make it
  • Tables that take bookings only in Korean, each one briefed on your guests' needs
The Temple Cuisine journey
iii.

Families, three generations

For families travelling with children or grandparents

Days paced to the youngest and the oldest, with every detail settled before arrival.

  • The palaces in hanbok, with a photographer who keeps a discreet distance
  • Private museum guides, and a quiet plan for every kind of weather
  • One driver throughout, with rest, mobility and diet considered from the start
Family Journeys
iv.

Milestones, made private

For proposals, honeymoons and anniversaries

The moments guests will carry for the rest of their lives, arranged in confidence.

  • A proposal in a private hanok courtyard, or a palace garden at its quietest hour
  • A honeymoon between Seoul's finest tables and a hanok in the mountains
  • Anniversaries and milestone birthdays, with a photographer if desired
Milestone Journeys

We also compose winter journeys, from first snow in Gangwon to the New Year in Seoul, and journeys for small groups.
