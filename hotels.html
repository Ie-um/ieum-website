Why we are writing

Our guests already stay with you. We would like to work beside your desk.

IEUM is a private travel concierge for Korea, between Seoul and New York. Guests travelling with us this winter are staying at Park Hyatt Seoul, and it made us ask a simple question: why not work with the hotel directly?

Your concierge team looks after the stay. Some requests reach further: three nights in the mountains of Gangwon, a temple stay, a week through Gyeongju and Jeju, a proposal in a private hanok. These take days to design and someone on the ground to run. That is the work we do, and only that.

When such a request reaches your desk, pass it to us. We design it, arrange it and stay with the guest throughout, then return them to you rested. There is no fee to the hotel and nothing for your team to administer.

The division

You keep the stay. We take what lies beyond it.

A clear line, so your team always knows what to hand over and what stays with the hotel.

Stays with you

The room, the spa, your restaurants, tables and tickets in the city, cars to the airport. We never rebook what your desk already does well, and we plan every journey around the guest's stay with you.

Comes to us

Journeys of a day or more beyond Seoul, wellness programs, temple stays, private cultural access, celebrations, and full itineraries for guests who ask before they arrive or wish to stay on in Korea.

Returns to you

The guest, and a short note to your desk on what they did and loved. When they extend their time in Seoul, we send them back to your hotel.

Working together

Four steps, one message from your desk

i.

Send the request

A message or an email with the guest's dates and what they asked for, shared with their consent. Nothing else is needed from your team.

ii.

We speak with the guest

We reply the same day, listen, and write a proposal day by day with a clear price. Your desk is copied if you wish.

iii.

We arrange and run it

Drivers, guides, interpreters, temples, tables and treatments, each confirmed in writing, with someone reachable in Korea at every hour.

iv.

We bring them back

The guest returns to your door, and your desk receives a short note on how it went.

The terms

Simple, and at no cost to the hotel

We ask for the introduction and nothing more.

No fee

Nothing is charged to the hotel or to your team. The guest contracts with IEUM and pays us directly, so there is no invoicing or liability for you to carry.

Your guest, always

We do not offer rooms in Seoul, and we never move a guest from your hotel. Where your policy provides for a referral arrangement, we follow it.

One contact

Your desk speaks to the founder directly. IEUM Concierge LLC is registered in New York, and our drivers in Korea are licensed and work under written contract.
