About

The connection between you and the real Korea

Ieum, 이음, means connection. To Korea's people, culture, and nature, and to the life you return to.

IEUM Concierge is a private luxury wellness travel concierge in Korea, planning, booking, and running bespoke journeys end to end for travellers from Europe, the United States, and Latin America. Wellness sits at the centre of every journey we design, woven through Korea's culture, beauty, and cuisine, whatever shape the trip takes: a family holiday, a milestone celebration, or time alone.

The result is a true vacation, the kind you do not need another vacation to recover from. No researching. No mapping out routes. No comparing options or chasing reservations. Every day is planned around you and handled for you, from the driver waiting at arrivals to the table held weeks in advance. You come home rested, refreshed, and rejuvenated.

One trusted point of contact. A network of vetted partners, from serene mountain temples to Seoul's most sought-after addresses. Every itinerary personally tested before it is ever recommended.

Experiences

Wellness, culture, and beauty across Korea

Wellness

Stillness, far from the city

Korean wellness travel across the country, from temple stays and hot spring towns to mountain hiking in Seoraksan.

Temple Stay in the Mountains

A night among monks in a centuries-old mountain temple, far from any city: meditation, tea, and true silence.

Hot Spring Retreat

Mineral-rich spring waters and slow days at Korea's most serene hot-spring resorts, finished with a luxury private dining experience.

Mountain & Valley Hiking

Trails that wind through Korea's most scenic valleys before rising to granite ridgelines: waterfalls, maple color, and quiet along the way.

Culture

Traditions, kept alive

Korean cultural experiences, from hanbok at the palaces to artisan workshops and private guided visits.

Royal Cuisine Cooking Class

Learn the refined dishes once served in Joseon palaces, guided by masters of Korea's royal culinary tradition.

The Haenyeo of Jeju

Meet the legendary free-diving women of Jeju Island and hear a living heritage told firsthand.

Traditional Craft Workshop

Two of Korea's living crafts, yours to choose: mother-of-pearl lacquerware guided by a Living National Treasure, or a soju and makgeolli brewing class taught by the masters keeping these traditions alive.

Beauty

Rejuvenation, refined

Korean beauty experiences, from hanbang facials and dermatology clinics to lash and styling studios.

Non-Invasive Treatments

Gentle, restorative skin rejuvenation at Korea's leading aesthetic clinics, from head spa and laser lifting to hydrotherapy and PDRN.

Beauty Enhancements

Refined, long-lasting beauty enhancements at Korea's most in-demand studios, from lash extensions and perms to microblading, lip blushing, and silk keratin treatments.

Makeup & Styling Consulting

A private 1:1 session with the makeup artists and stylists behind Korea's A-list celebrities, finding the colors and style made for you, then a photo shoot to remember it by.
