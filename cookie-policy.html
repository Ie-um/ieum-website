Cookie Policy

What we set, what each thing is for, and how to change your mind at any time.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work at all. Others record how a site is used, or support advertising. This page sets out which of those we use and why.

This policy applies to ie-um.com. It was last updated on 23 September 2026.

Your choice

Nothing beyond the strictly necessary is set until you allow it. If you are visiting from the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked before anything else loads. You can change your choice whenever you like.

Strictly necessary

These keep the site working and remember the choice you made here. They are not used to track you and cannot be switched off.

Things that are not cookies

Two parts of this site load content from elsewhere, and it is fair to say so.

Booking

Consultations are booked through Cal.com. Opening the booking window loads Cal.com in the page, and Cal.com may set its own cookies. It is only loaded when you open it.

Fonts and images

Typefaces come from Google Fonts and some photographs are served from other sites. These set no cookies, although the provider will see that a request was made.

Country

We ask our own server which country a request came from, so we know whether to show the cookie notice. Cloudflare resolves this at our edge. No third party is involved and no address is stored.

Turning cookies off in your browser

Every browser lets you block or delete cookies in its own settings, independently of anything on this page. Blocking the strictly necessary ones may stop parts of the site working properly.
