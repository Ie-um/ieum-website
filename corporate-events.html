Corporate travel and event management in Korea

Wellness led retreats and incentive travel for your own team, and small client facing events in Korea for brands. Both staged against a distinctly Korean setting and run on the ground by us.

The Idea

Why companies bring their teams to Korea

Korea has become one of the more compelling destinations for corporate travel, and not only because of the flight connections. It offers a rare combination for a company: world class conference infrastructure in Seoul, genuinely distinctive cultural experiences within a short drive, and a hospitality standard that consistently surprises visiting teams. The same is true for brands. A house wanting to gather its Korean clients has access here to hanok courtyards, private museum rooms and chefs who do not otherwise cater, and almost none of it is bookable from abroad.

What Korea does not offer, if you are organizing from abroad, is easy access. The best venues are often booked through relationships rather than websites. Contracts, invoicing, and supplier communication happen in Korean. Group rates at leading hotels are negotiated, not published. A great deal of what makes an event work here is invisible from the outside.

That is where we come in. IEUM works as a corporate event management agency inside Korea, planning and running the event on the ground so your team is not managing timezones, translation, and vendor chasing from another continent.

We work with companies bringing anywhere from a dozen to several hundred people, and with brands hosting rooms of eight to eighty, across Seoul, Busan, Jeju, and the regions between.

Two Directions

The two ways companies work with us

One brings your own people to Korea. The other brings your clients to you, here. They are planned differently, and we run both.

One

Corporate travel

For your team. Wellness sits at the centre of the programme, the same as it does in every journey we design, so people come home restored instead of needing a week to recover.

Executive retreats

Smaller senior groups, usually three to five days, balancing working sessions with the kind of setting that makes difficult conversations easier. Often a private estate or a hanok property outside the city, with a hot spring or a temple morning built into the schedule and meals designed as part of the programme.

Incentive travel

Reward trips for top performers, where the experience has to justify the achievement. Korean beauty and wellness programmes, private cultural access, and dining that people photograph before they eat.

Off-sites and team travel

Larger groups needing rooms, meeting space, transport, and something memorable in the evenings. We handle the logistics so your internal team can be present rather than running the day.

Two

Client facing events for brands

For luxury houses with clients, press or partners in Korea. Small rooms, carefully chosen, where the setting is the point. We source the venue, stage the evening, and run it on the night.

Private client dinners

Eight to thirty guests, in a room that is not otherwise available: a hanok courtyard, a museum after closing, a chef's counter taken in full. Seating, menu, interpretation and gifting handled, and a Korean host present so the evening runs in the right register.

Launches, previews and VIP hosting

A collection preview, a market entry moment, a press and partner evening, or a visiting founder to look after for two days. We arrange the space, the production, the guides and the transport, and we brief everyone on the floor before your guests arrive.
