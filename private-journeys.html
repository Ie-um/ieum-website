The Idea

What a private journey with IEUM actually means

Most travel to Korea follows the same handful of paths. The palaces, the shopping districts, the restaurants that appear on every list. Those places are worth seeing, and we will take you to them. But a private journey is built the other way around, starting with you.

Before anything is booked, we want to understand how you travel. Whether mornings are for slow breakfasts or early starts. Whether you want a skincare clinic in Gangnam or a temple stay in the mountains. Whether you are traveling with children, with a partner, or entirely alone and glad of it. Every itinerary we design begins with that conversation, and nothing is copied from the last one.

What follows is a journey where the logistics disappear. A driver who knows where to be. A table held at a restaurant that does not take online reservations. A guide who can explain why a particular hanok was built facing that direction. And a single point of contact who answers when something changes, because something always does.

We work across Seoul, Jeju, Busan, and the quieter regions in between, in every season, for stays from a few days to a few weeks.

The Process

How your journey comes together

i.

A conversation

We begin with a call, usually half an hour. We ask about the trip you have in mind, who is traveling, what you have already seen of Korea, and what you would rather avoid. Most of the useful detail comes out of what people mention in passing.

ii.

A designed itinerary

We build your journey day by day and send it to you as a private page, with each day laid out, the reasoning behind the pacing explained, and photographs of the places you will actually go. You see exactly what your trip will feel like before you commit to any of it.

iii.

Refinement

Nothing is fixed on the first draft. You tell us what you love and what you would change, and we rebuild around it. This usually takes a round or two, and we would rather you say so now than be politely disappointed later.

iv.

Your journey

Once confirmed, we secure every reservation, driver, and experience. Each evening of your trip you receive a briefing for the following day, sent however you prefer, and we remain reachable throughout your stay.

Experiences

Beauty, wellness, and culture

Three threads that run through most of the journeys we design, though never in the same proportion twice.

Korean beauty, at the source

Skincare consultations with practitioners who work with Korea's leading brands, hanbang facials rooted in traditional herbal medicine, and dermatology clinics that take non invasive treatment seriously. Booked properly, with a translator present, so nothing is lost in the consultation.

Wellness, far from the city

Hot spring towns in the mountains, temple stays where you rise before dawn, jjimjilbang traditions that Koreans have kept for generations, and spa retreats that most visitors never find because they are not written up in English.

Culture, kept alive

Palaces in hanbok, artisan workshops with makers still practicing techniques passed down through families, private museum visits with a guide, and meals in restaurants that have served the same dish for three generations.
